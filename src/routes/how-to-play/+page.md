# How to Play Kings in the Corner

I grew up having family game nights with my parents and siblings.
One of the games we played was Kings in the Corner.
I find the simple strategy and randomness of the game to be endlessly entertaining.

## Goal

Be the first player to get rid of all of your cards.

## Turn flow

On your turn:

1. You the player will go first.
2. Click the "Draw" Card button to add a card to your hand.
3. You can play any valid card from your hand onto the piles on the board.
4. If you have a King, you can place it in one of the four corner piles.
5. You can play as many cards as you want, as long as they are valid moves.
6. Once you are done playing cards, click the "End Turn" button to end your turn.

The computer then takes its turn.

## Valid moves

You can play a card onto a pile if it is the opposite color and one rank lower than the top card of that pile.

Example Valid Moves:

- Queen of Spades (Black) can be played on top of a King of Hearts (Red).
- King of any suit can be played in an empty corner pile.
- Top card of a pile is a 5 of Diamonds (Red), you can move it to a corner pile with a 6 of Clubs (Black) on top of it.

## Tips

- If a pile is empty, try to play higher ranked cards. If you place a Ace you will not be able to play any cards on that pile.
- You might want to keep a King in your hand to prevent the computer from playing it's cards.
