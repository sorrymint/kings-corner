# How to Play Kings in the Corner

Kings in the Corner is a simple card game for 1 player against the computer.

## Goal

Be the first player to get rid of all of your cards.

## Setup

- The deck is shuffled and dealt into the player hand, the computer hand, and the center piles.
- The board has a few face-up piles around the center.
- The stock pile is placed in the middle.

## Turn flow

On your turn:

1. Draw a card from the stock if you have not already drawn this turn.
2. Play one card from your hand onto a valid pile.
3. Or move a pile onto another valid pile.
4. End your turn.

The computer then takes its turn.

## Valid moves

You can play a card onto a pile if it matches the same suit or is one rank higher or lower, depending on the game rule in use.

In this version, the game checks for legal moves automatically, and invalid plays are rejected.

## Corner piles

The corner piles are special spaces where you can place cards to build toward your win.

## Winning

When either your hand or the computer hand is empty, the game ends. The player with no cards remaining wins.

## Tips

- Try to keep options open in the middle of the board.
- Watch the computer’s moves so you can predict the best play.
- Use the corner piles carefully when the main piles get crowded.
